# Starter tags by team type

Setup proposes 12 to 20 starter tags from the set that fits the team, each with its one-line meaning, so a new book starts with a shared vocabulary. Pick the closest set, mix in tags drawn from any material the user attached, and let the user edit the list before writing it into settings. Copy the lines as they are: they are already in the settings format.

## General team

- onboarding: first 30 days of a client or hire
- customers: what customers say, want and complain about
- pricing: what customers pay, objections, discounts
- process: how we run recurring work
- tooling: software and workarounds
- meetings: how we run meetings and what comes out of them
- communication: how we write updates and explain things
- planning: goals, priorities and plans
- hiring: finding, interviewing and welcoming people
- retros: what we learn when we look back on work
- quality: mistakes caught, checks and standards
- suppliers: vendors, partners and contracts
- costs: spending, budgets and savings
- handoff: passing work between people or teams

## Agency (design, web, marketing)

- client-onboarding: the first 30 days with a new client
- scope: what is in and out of a project, and scope creep
- pricing: what clients pay, objections, discounts
- estimates: how we size work and how estimates land
- handoff: passing a finished site or asset to the client
- accessibility: making our work usable for everyone
- performance: page speed and site performance
- cms: content management systems and editing content
- hosting: domains, hosting and launches
- tooling: software, plugins and workarounds
- hiring: finding, interviewing and welcoming new people
- process: how we run projects day to day
- design-feedback: giving, getting and acting on design feedback
- retainer: ongoing support and retainer agreements
- communication: how we talk with clients and with each other

## Operations

- onboarding: first 30 days of a new hire or site
- suppliers: vendors, orders and contracts

- logistics: deliveries, shipping and returns
- safety: hazards, near misses and safe ways of working
- quality: defects, checks and standards

- costs: spending, budgets and savings
- incidents: what went wrong and how we recovered
- maintenance: equipment, repairs and upkeep
- process: how we run recurring work
- tooling: software and workarounds

## Customer support and service desk

- onboarding: a customer's first weeks with us
- billing: invoices, payments and refunds
- bugs: faults customers report and how we confirm them
- how-to: questions about using the product or service
- escalation: when and how a case goes to another team
- response-time: how fast we answer and what slows us down
- tone: wording that calms or upsets customers
- saved-replies: reply templates and when they work
- known-issues: problems we know about and their workarounds
- feature-requests: what customers ask us to add
- churn: why customers leave or nearly leave
- self-service: help articles and answers customers find alone
- tooling: software and workarounds
- handoff: passing a case between people or shifts

## Product and research

- user-research: interviews, surveys and what users told us
- usability: where people get stuck using the product
- onboarding: a new user's first sessions
- feature-requests: what users ask for and why
- pricing: what users pay, objections, discounts
- competitors: what other products do and how users compare us
- experiments: tests we ran and what they showed
- metrics: numbers we track and what moved them
- roadmap: what we plan to build and why
- positioning: how we describe the product and to whom
- retention: why users stay or leave
- accessibility: making the product usable for everyone
- performance: speed and reliability
- bugs: faults found and how we fixed them
- launches: releasing features and what we learned
