# Larchmont Studio commonplace book

This folder is our team commonplace book: a numbered log of the customer quotes, lessons, fixes, insights and decisions worth keeping. Each entry has a source and a reason, so anyone on the team can find it again and trust it.

## Three rules

1. One fragment per entry: one quote, one lesson, one fix.
2. Always a source: what it came from, who said it (a role or a company) and when.
3. Always why it matters: one sentence on why the team should keep it.

## How to add an entry

- Paste the material into Claude and say "add to the commonplace".
- Or use the Commonplace Starter web app, then export entries.md into this folder.
- Or copy the last entry block in entries.md by hand, give it the next ID and fill in every line.

Don't edit index-tags.md, index-themes.md or sources.md. They are rebuilt from the entries every time Claude adds to the book.

## What else is in this folder

- `weeks` holds the week notes from closing the week.
- `projects` holds a page for each project the team starts or closes.
- `briefs` holds the briefs Claude writes when someone asks what the book knows about a topic.

Claude makes each folder the first time it needs it.

## Who curates

Operations lead (rotates quarterly) looks after the book: reviewing new entries, merging duplicates and retiring what is out of date. Ask them about tags or anything that looks wrong.
