# Call notes: Ombrell Pottery, 2026-11-02

First call with Ottoline Varrow, who owns Ombrell Pottery. They run evening and weekend classes and sell bowls and mugs from a small shop in the workshop. Notes by the project manager.

## What they want

- A new site where people can see class dates, book a place and buy from the shop.
- To change class dates and prices themselves.

## What she told us

- "I want people to feel they have walked into the workshop, not into a catalogue." She means warm photos of real classes, not product shots on white.
- She has had quotes with ranges before and felt caught out when the bill came. She wants one number she can plan around.
- Most of their students are retired, and several find the small pale text on the current site hard to read. She asked for larger text and clear buttons on the class pages.
- She is not sure who controls the domain. Her nephew set it up years ago and may still hold the account. We need that settled before we agree a launch date.

## Onboarding

- I said she will have one named contact for the first month, which will be me. She was pleased.
- She asked for one list of everything we need from her: photos of the pieces, class descriptions, teacher profiles and shop prices.

## Contact details

- Ottoline's mobile is 07700 900461, best after four on weekdays.
- Her personal email is ottoline.varrow@example.net. She would rather keep this project out of the shop inbox.

## Next steps

- Send the content checklist and kickoff call times by Friday.
- Ask her to check with her nephew about the domain this week.
- Draft an estimate with a single expected figure.
